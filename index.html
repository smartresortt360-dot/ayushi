A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I’m Ayushi.

I’m a 3rd-year B.Tech Data Science student who discovered an interest in the creative world alongside my technical studies.

While Data Science has taught me to think logically and work with information, I’ve also found myself drawn towards content, social media and the way ideas connect with people.

I enjoy exploring trends, observing what makes content interesting, and thinking about how a simple idea can be presented in a way that feels relatable and engaging.

I’m still learning and building my skills, and that’s exactly what makes me excited about working in a real-world environment. I’m looking for an opportunity where I can contribute my creativity, learn from experienced people and grow through practical experience.
